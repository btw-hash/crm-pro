fix(ui) Collapsed rail: bring back the cream "scoop" for the active item on the slim strip

What changed

In the previous fix (the slim strip), the active item was left without a "scoop" — the disc simply stuck out with a round edge. The cream "scoop" cut-out is restored (reference design "CRM Pro UI"): a cream S-curve in the background color is cut into the right edge of the dark slim capsule, and the dark disc pops out of it. The scoop, disc, and glyph move vertically together when switching between menu items (a CSS transition on transform), so the active-indicator animation is preserved.

Before → after

Element Before (slim, no scoop) After
Active-item indicator Dark disc with a round edge on the strip's straight edge Cream "scoop" cut-out + dark disc popping out of it
Animation between items Only the disc moves Scoop + disc + glyph move together (smooth indicator travel)

The dark strip and its width were untouched — only the cream cut-out layer was restored.

Repro steps

  1. Sign in as Head of Sales or Manager (in dev, localhost opens as Head of Sales).
  2. Collapse the side menu into the rail (hamburger icon at the top of the sidebar).
  3. On the active item ("Home") see the cream "scoop" cut-out at the edge of the strip, with the dark disc bearing the house icon popping out of it.
  4. Click another section on the rail → the scoop and disc smoothly travel to the new item.
