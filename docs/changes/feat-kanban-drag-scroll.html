The "Tasks" board: drag-to-scroll horizontally

branch feat/kanban-drag-scroll · requested by the client

What was added

On the deals board (kanban) you can now scroll the columns sideways by grabbing an empty spot (the board background, a column body, a stage header) and dragging — like the "hand" tool in maps/Figma. The cursor on the board is a "hand" (grab / grabbing while dragging).

Card drag-and-drop is NOT broken

The two gestures are split by what you grab:

  • grabbed an empty spot (not a card) → the board pan-scrolls;
  • grabbed a card → the existing drag-and-drop between stages (native, no changes).

Under the hood: a mousedown on the board starts the pan only if the target isn't [draggable]/a button/a link/a control (otherwise — it bails out, staying out of the way of DnD/clicks/opening a card). The move is tracked on window (the pan doesn't "drop" when the cursor leaves the board).

Repro steps

  1. Open /deals ("Tasks").
  2. Hover an empty spot on the board (background/stage header) — the cursor is a "hand".
  3. Hold the left mouse button and drag sideways — the columns scroll horizontally.
  4. Grab a card and drag it to another stage — the move works as before.
  5. Click a card's title — the side panel opens (as before).

Verified in the browser: panning on an empty spot moved scrollLeft 0→180; a mousedown on a card left scrollLeft=0 (DnD wasn't hijacked); cards remain draggable. npm run verify green (789 tests).
