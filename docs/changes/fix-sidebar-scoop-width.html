fix(ui) Narrowing the active-item scoop cut-out on the rail

What changed

On the collapsed rail, the cream "scoop" cut-out (cove) around the active icon was noticeably wider than the reference design: it went 40px deep at a rail width of 78px (eating up half of it) and stretched 140px tall (~2.3 rows). Brought back in line with the reference — a thin, neat cove.

There was no regression: the cut-out's geometry has been unchanged since the rail's first commit (ac8f064, June 27) — the implementation was simply built wider than the reference from the start.

Before → after

Layer Before After
Cut-out (SVG scoop) 46×140, 40px deep 34×92, 22px deep
Active-item disc 56px, 28px protrusion 50px, 22px protrusion
Glyph (icon on the disc) 24px 22px

All three layers were recalculated consistently (shift() for the new sizes), vertical centering on the row is preserved.

Repro steps

  1. Open the app under any role → navigate to "Home" (/).
  2. Collapse the side menu into the rail (hamburger icon at the top of the sidebar).
  3. See the active item "Home": the dark disc with the house icon in a thin cream cove at the rail's right edge — no wide white "blob".
  4. Navigate to another section from the rail → the cove and disc smoothly travel to the new item, the cut-out stays just as thin.
