Fix for the sidebar collapse animation (#391 [2])

Before: when collapsing the sidebar panel, the panel content disappeared instantly, while the floating active-item indicator (the white "scoop" cut-out + the dark disc with the icon) hung in the middle of the still-wide dark box, with an empty dark area gaping on the right — while the width animated 248→78px. It looked broken.

After: during the width animation, the indicator's floating layers are hidden (opacity: discOn && !animating) and fade in smoothly once the collapse finishes. The box collapses cleanly — the icon column remains on the left, with no "blob" and no empty dark trailing area. Symmetric to the panel appearing when expanding.

The fix is surgical: the three indicator layers (scoop / disc / glyph) in app-sidebar.tsx — an animating gate was added plus an opacity .2s fade in the transition.

Verification

  1. Open any screen, expand the sidebar (menu icon).
  2. Collapse the sidebar → the panel collapses smoothly, with no indicator stuck in the middle of the box.
  3. In the collapsed state, the active-item indicator is back in place.
