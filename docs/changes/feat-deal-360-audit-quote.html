360 deal card — "Audit" and "Proposal" workspaces (PR6, step 6e)

The final two stages of the 360 deal card have been brought in line with the unified bento style (like Qualification/Testing). No reference mockup exists for these stages — they were designed following the approved pattern used by the other workspaces.

Before: the "Production audit" and "Proposal" sections were bare forms with no framing, out of step with the rest of the card.

After:

  • Audit — a bento workspace: an intro ("record the equipment and client pain points"), the audit form in a glass card; once the stage is passed, the filled-in values become read-only.
  • Proposal — a bento workspace: a "Proposal" card (deterministic calculation + AI-assisted drafting) and a "Client letter" card, both in glass; once the stage is passed — "Proposal stage completed".

These are presentational wrappers around existing forms (AuditForm / ProposalComposer / ClientMessageComposer) — domain logic, calculations, and RBAC were not changed.

Verification

  1. Open a deal at the "Audit" stage → the "Production audit" section shows the intro and form in a glass card.
  2. Open a deal at the "Proposal" stage → two cards: Proposal and "Client letter".
  3. Open a completed deal → "Production audit · completed" (read-only values) and "Proposal · completed".
