AI assistant: chat + estimated request cost

The conversation now builds up as a thread; every reply shows the AI spend

What was done (closes #216)

  • The Head of Sales's assistant went from a single "question-answer" to a chat: the conversation builds up as a thread (the history of questions and answers is visible).
  • For every reply — a cost estimate for the request (≈ tokens and ≈ $) and, at the bottom, a running total of AI spend.
  • Answers are still built strictly from CRM data (numbers come from the CRM deterministically, the AI doesn't make them up), with a link to the source snapshot.
  • The cost is an estimate (marked "≈"), for keeping an eye on spend; the exact token count comes from the provider.

How to verify

  1. Sign in as Alan Peterson (Head of Sales), open "Assistant".
  2. Ask a question (e.g. "Who's over their credit limit?"), then a second one ("Show me the workload by rep").
  3. Both question-answer pairs stay in the thread; under each answer — the source and "≈ N tokens · ≈ $X".
  4. At the bottom — a total: "Spent (estimate): ≈ … tokens · ≈ $… across N requests".
