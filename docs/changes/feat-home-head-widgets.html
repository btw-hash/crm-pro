Head of sales home page — "Revenue by period" + "Full report"

feat/home-head-widgets · Closes #435 · design §8.

1. "Revenue by period" widget with a Month / Quarter / Year switch

Before

On the head of sales home page, revenue showed only last month's figure as a single number — with no breakdown by period.

After

A separate widget with a Month / Quarter / Year switch and graphite bars of revenue by period (darker ≠ higher — a single accent graphite).

The numbers are actual revenue (a deterministic aggregation of the ERP's monthly history), no invented "plan": there's no revenue target in $ in the system, so we show the actuals.

2. The "Full report →" link

Before

There was no one-click way from the home page into the team's full analytics.

After

The "Full report →" link from the widget leads to the "Analytics" section.

How to see it

Repro steps

  1. Open the home page as the head of sales role (the default on localhost).
  2. Find the "Revenue by period" widget (next to "Team pipeline").
  3. Switch between Month / Quarter / Year → the bars and totals rebuild for the period.
  4. Click "Full report →" → "Analytics" opens.
