Deals: Won/Lost outcome, conversion, and RBAC

fix(deals) · closes #370 · refs #317 · branch fix/deals-outcome-rbac

A group of demo bugs from the QA audit in the deals area: "Funnel conversion" = 0%, the outcome of completed deals is nowhere visible, several role-access holes, and minor glitches in stage progress and tech requests. Only files in the deals area and prisma/seed.ts are affected.

What changed

  • Conversion 0% → real. prisma/seed.ts seeded a deal without the outcome field → on the postgres demo every completed deal had outcome = NULL → win-rate/"Funnel conversion" = 0. Now outcome is written in both the create and update upsert (the won/lost values were already in seed/deals.ts).
  • Outcome visible everywhere. A Won / Lost badge on the deal card, on the kanban board, and in the board's side drawer (single source DEAL_OUTCOME_LABELS).
  • RBAC #7. Accountant/logistics/technologist/category manager could see the entire deal portfolio. The /deals section and the /deals/[id] card are now available only to sales roles (head of sales + manager); everyone else → 404.
  • RBAC #10. The client dropdown in the deal create/edit forms gave the manager the entire counterparty base → narrowed to the manager's own owner scope.
  • RBAC #27. A manager could assign a deal to another manager → server-side validation (a manager can only assign to themselves, the head of sales can assign to anyone) in createDealAction/updateDealAction/inlineUpdateDealAction + narrowed the owner dropdown in the UI.
  • RBAC #8. requestTechAction (tech request) didn't check deal access → added the same owner boundary (dealAccess) as the other actions.
  • #28. The "Proposal calculation" section was marked "done" for the lost REF-5 deal, even though that stage was skipped (the deal was closed bypassing it). For lost deals, skipped stages are no longer marked as done.
  • #29. Seeded tech requests (tr-ref-4, tr-ref-3) weren't shown on the deal card → now they render with a status (Open/In progress).
