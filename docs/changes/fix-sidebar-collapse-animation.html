#351 · Visual artifact during the sidebar collapse animation

The white "gap" around the active item during menu collapse — fixed.

What changed

In the collapsed sidebar, the active item is highlighted by a "blob" — a scoop cut-out in the page background color (#F8F3EF) that appears to "bite into" the dark sidebar. When collapsing, the dark box's width animates 248 → 78px, while the cut-out's geometry is computed for the final 78px. During the transition, the cut-out momentarily ended up inside the still-wide dark box and was visible as a crooked white blob on the dark background. Now, during the animation, the cut-out is neatly clipped by the right edge of the narrowing box and appears in sync with the width — no gap.

Before → after (live demo)

Click "Collapse/Expand" under each panel. On the left — the bug (a white blob flashes on the dark background during the collapse); on the right — the fix (the cut-out smoothly follows the edge).

Before → after (the fix in essence)

Before

The dark box instantly got overflow: visible, while the width was still animating 248→78. The scoop cut-out (geometry sized for 78px) "hung" inside the wide box as a white blob until the animation finished — a crooked white gap around the active icon.

After

For the duration of the width animation, the box holds overflow: hidden (the animating flag, lifted after 420ms). The cut-out/disc are clipped by the box edge and appear in sync with the width. visible — only once the collapsed box has settled. The end states are unchanged.

Repro steps (in the app)

  1. Open the app, navigate to any section (the active item is highlighted by a "blob").
  2. Collapse the sidebar (the "menu" icon) and watch the active icon right during the animation.
  3. Confirm: there is no white gap/crooked blob around the active item — the cut-out smoothly follows the width.
  4. Expand back — the transition is clean too. The end states look the same as before.
