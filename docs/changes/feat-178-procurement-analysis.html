Assortment analytics — procurement analysis

What to reorder, what to hold, what to cut back — by depletion rate, not by stock level

What was done (closes #178 and the entire epic #176)

  • The "Assortment" page gets a second section — "Procurement" (alongside the sales analysis from #177): for each item, a reorder / hold / cut back signal.
  • Under-stocked → depleting fast, stock is running out → "reorder" with a recommended quantity; overstocked → sitting there, cash is tied up → "cut back on ordering".
  • The key metric is months of stock (stock level ÷ sales velocity), not the raw stock level — it accounts for depletion/velocity.
  • Introduced a purchasing/receiving model (`getPurchaseHistory` behind the ERP port) — a synthetic seed for the demo; in production it's pulled from the ERP. The numbers are deterministic, AI doesn't take part (data guardrail #179).
