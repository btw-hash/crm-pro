#306 · Board task card without quick-action buttons

The board is now clean: action buttons removed from the cards. Stage changes happen by dragging or on the full card.

What changed

The board cards no longer show the "+ Activity", "⏭ Next step", "⇄ Change stage", "↪ Hand off" buttons, or the expandable stage-transition buttons ("→ Test request", "→ Proposal", "↩ Qualification", etc). These actions remain on the full task card /deals/[id]. Changing stage right on the board — drag the card between columns (as before).

Before → after

Before

Every card had a row of action and stage-transition buttons underneath; cards looked visually cluttered.

After

The card shows only the title, tags, and key fields. Actions live on the full card; stage change is by dragging.

Repro steps

  1. Open the "Tasks" section → the board cards have no Activity/Next step/Change stage/Hand off buttons.
  2. Drag a card to the neighboring column → the stage changes (forward — instantly, backward — with a reason confirmation).
  3. Open the full task card → the actions (stage, next step, activity) are available there.
