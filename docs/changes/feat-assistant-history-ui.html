Head of sales AI assistant: conversation history PR2 of 2

History panel · message search · "New conversation" · dialogue context · clickable sources

What changed

  • "History" panel to the left of the chat: a list of the head of sales's conversations (title + date, newest first), loaded when the page opens. Clicking a conversation opens its messages in the chat.
  • Search across history: a search field with a ~250ms debounce searches message text and shows matching conversations with a match snippet.
  • "New conversation": the button clears the chat and starts a new conversation.
  • Conversation memory: every exchange (question + answer) is saved on the server; continuing the conversation appends to the same conversation, and previous turns are passed to the assistant as context for follow-ups ("any links?", "any other options?").
  • Clickable sources: web results are now actual links (the company name is the link text, opens in a new tab), not just red text.

Access is limited to the head of sales role (head). All history is private — per owner (server-side RBAC), other people's conversations aren't shown. Closes #340 · backend — #339.

Repro steps
  1. Log in as the head of sales (role head) and open the "AI assistant" section.
  2. Ask a question (e.g. "Find new ingredient suppliers") → get an answer. A new conversation with a title from the question appears in the "History" panel on the left.
  3. If the answer has web sources — click the company name: the source's site opens in a new tab.
  4. Ask a follow-up question ("any links?") — the assistant answers in the context of the previous exchange, the turn is appended to the same conversation.
  5. Click "+ New conversation" — the chat clears, a new conversation starts.
  6. In the "Search history" field, type a word from a past question — the list narrows to matching conversations with a snippet; clear the field → the full list returns.
  7. Click any conversation in the panel — its messages load into the chat, and any continuation is appended there.
