fix(ui) Collapsed rail: slim capsule + protruding disc instead of the cream cut-out

What changed

The collapsed rail looked like a wide dark rectangle (150px), where the active item was marked by a cream "scoop" cut-out. The cut-out blended into the page background, and the disc "floated" inside the wide block. Replaced with a slim capsule: a narrow rounded dark strip, with a dark disc protruding from its right edge on the active item (the left half blends into the rail, the right half sticks out). The cream cut-out is removed.

Cause of the wide rectangle: the .app-sidebar class (a leftover from the old CSS sidebar) painted the entire 150px column dark on top of the rounded 78px inner capsule — that capsule was invisible (dark on dark). In the collapsed state the outer shell is now transparent (the background/clip from the class are kept for the expanded panel and the mobile off-canvas).

Before → after

Element Before After
Rail shape Wide dark rectangle, 150px Slim capsule, 78px, outer shell transparent
Active-item indicator Cream "scoop" cut-out + disc inside Dark disc protrudes past the capsule's right edge
Column width (collapsed) 150px 116px (content sits closer to the rail)

Repro steps

  1. Sign in as Head of Sales or Manager (in dev, localhost opens as Head of Sales).
  2. Collapse the side menu into the rail (hamburger icon at the top of the sidebar).
  3. See a narrow dark capsule with centered icons; on the active item ("Home") the dark disc with the house icon protrudes to the right past the capsule's edge — no cream cut-out.
  4. Navigate to another section from the rail → the disc smoothly travels to the new item.
  5. Expand the menu (hamburger) → the full dark panel is unchanged.
