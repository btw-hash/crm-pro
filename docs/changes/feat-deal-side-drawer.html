Side drawer — the deal card as a right-hand panel

Clicking a deal opens it in a slide-out panel; the list stays visible on the left

What changed (#156, demo UX TASK-4)

  • Clicking a deal's name in the pipeline opens it in a right-hand panel (drawer) — with no navigation to a separate page, the board stays visible on the left.
  • Deep link / URL: the open card is reflected in the address (/deals?deal=<id>) — the link can be shared, or the page reloaded, and the drawer opens on its own.
  • Closing via Esc and by clicking outside the panel — the URL returns to the list (other filters, e.g. ?view=, are preserved).
  • Inside — the Score Pill (deal health), key fields (stage / manager / client / goal), the next step, a short activity feed, and an "Open in full" button leading to the full card.
  • Respects RBAC: a sales rep can't open someone else's deal even via a direct link — the drawer will show "unavailable".

Why (vs legacy CRMs)

The most "modern" pattern from Attio / Linear / Intercom: you review several records in a row without losing the list's context. a legacy CRM does a full page reload on every deal navigation; here the list never goes away, and the card slides out on top of it. The drawer is deliberately focused (not 15 sections of a heavy page) — a quick overview, with an "Open in full" for the details.

Before → after

Opening a deal Before: click → a full navigation to the page /deals/<id>, the board is lost.
After: click → a right-hand panel, the board stays on the left.
Reviewing several deals Before: back-and-forth "open → back → open the next".
After: close the drawer (Esc) — the list is right there, open the next one.
Link to a deal Before: only the full page.
After: /deals?deal=<id> — opens the drawer right in the list's context.

How to verify

  1. Open /deals — the pipeline of deals.
  2. Click any deal's name — the card panel slides out on the right.
  3. Confirm that the list stays visible on the left, and ?deal=<id> appears in the address.
  4. Press Esc — the drawer closes, the address is clean again.
  5. Open it again and click outside the panel (on the dimmed area) — it also closes.
  6. Copy the address with ?deal=<id> and reload the page — the drawer opens right away (deep link).
  7. Log in as a sales rep and open a direct link to someone else's deal — the drawer shows "unavailable".
