Role-based "Home" — "what should I do right now"

A starting dashboard of bento tiles instead of a generic list of tables

What was done (closes #190, at the designer's request)

  • On sign-in, every role now lands on "Home" — a dashboard of bento tiles that answers "what should I do right now", instead of a generic client list.
  • The client list moved to the "Clients" section (a "Home" item was added to the sidebar).
  • The tiles are populated with existing data (numbers from the ERP, deterministic, no AI involved); for a sales rep, the owner boundary is respected — they only see their own.

A dedicated "Home" per role

Role "What to do now" tiles
Sales Rep My day (overdue/today's tasks) · Urgent deals · My pipeline · My revenue
Head of Sales Team (workload/stalled deals by rep) · Needs attention · Team pipeline and revenue · Assistant
Technologist Request queue (new/in progress, by type) · Mine in progress — no commercial figures
Buyer Procurement: under-stocked/overstocked · Assortment (grow/stop) · Operational queues (packing/defects/onboarding)
