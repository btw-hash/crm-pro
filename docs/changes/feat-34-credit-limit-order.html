Deferred-payment credit limit in orders

You can't sell "on credit" to someone who's already over their limit

What was done (closes #34)

  • On the deal screen, before placing a "Customer order," the client's balance is visible: available deferred-payment limit, current AR (accounts receivable), and credit limit.
  • If the order amount pushes the client over the deferred-payment limit — placing the order "on credit" is blocked with an exact message (by how much it's exceeded).
  • A manual override is available ("place over limit" at your own responsibility) — it goes through, but is logged loudly.
  • The numbers come from the ERP (balance + individual pricing), the decision is deterministic (AI is not involved). Visible only to sales; the technologist doesn't see the balance/order.

Before → after

Placing an order Before After
Debt control The order was created without regard to AR/limit An order over the deferred-payment limit is blocked
Balance visibility AR/limit — only in the client card Available limit is visible right at the order form
Exceptions — Manual override is possible, but logged

How to verify

  1. Log in as Alan Peterson (Head of sales), open the won deal deal-6 (client "Union Grocery Wholesale").
  2. In the "Customer order (ERP)" block you can see: "Deferred-payment limit: $9,000 available · AR $13,000 of $22,000".
  3. Select Noirelle, quantity 1500, a date → "Create order." A block appears: "Order for $11,500 exceeds the available deferred-payment limit of $9,000… Placing the order 'on credit' is blocked."
  4. Check "Place over limit" → "Create order anyway" → the order is created (No. STUB-…); the override is logged.
  5. An order within the limit (e.g. 500 units) goes through immediately, with no warning.
