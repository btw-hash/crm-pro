1

Sales rep quotas — before / after

Before

Quotas did not exist. A manager could work with any volume with no personal reserve. Plan limits lived only in the ERP.

After

The entity Quota { mopId, productId, period, plannedVolume, reservedVolume, soldVolume }. The reserveFromQuota function throws QuotaError when the available volume is exceeded — a hard P2 guard. The table on /procurement shows plan / reserved / sold / available per sales rep and product. Rows with remaining stock below 15% of plan are highlighted red with a warning.

2

Deficit scoring — before / after

Before

When stock ran short, allocation was manual, "by arrangement" — subjective and opaque to the category manager.

After

A deterministic scoreRequest formula (0–100 points):

Factor Weight Mapping
Client status 40 A=40, B=25, C=10
Need type 30 production_stop=30, scheduled=18, trial=6
Shelf life 20 normalized over 0–30 days (0 days = 20 points)
Margin 10 normalized over 0–100%

allocateDeficit: a greedy algorithm, min(requested, remaining) in descending score order. The category manager sees the proposed allocation and its justification — and confirms it manually (passive capture).

Step-by-step reproduction in the browser

  1. Start the app: npm run dev in the teammate/ folder, open http://localhost:3000.
  2. Log in as any sales rep: username owens / password demo1234.
  3. In the sidebar navigation click Procurement (a new item with a 📦 icon).
  4. Confirm that the "Quotas by sales rep" table shows 6 rows: Owens (Belcolade, S500), Grant (Topfil, Mimetic), Mills (Sapore, Carat Cover).
  5. Check the Available column: Mills' remaining Sapore stock is 30 kg (150 plan − 30 reserved − 140 sold = −20? no, 200−30−140=30) — a row with no warning; Grant's Mimetic (150−70−75=5 kg, <15% of 150) — red color and a ⚠ symbol.
  6. Scroll down to the Deficit proposal — Topfil Lingonberry block: 150 kg available.
  7. Confirm that the 4 requests are sorted by descending score: the first is client A / production_stop (highest score), the last is client C / trial.
  8. Check the Allocated, kg column: the first request (80 kg) is green (in full), the second (120 kg, but only 70 kg remaining) is orange (partial), the rest are red (0).
  9. The Justification column shows the score breakdown per factor.
