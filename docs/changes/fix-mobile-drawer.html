Mobile menu: an off-canvas drawer instead of a narrow rail

Branch fix/mobile-drawer · desktop view untouched.

What changed

On a phone, the side menu collapsed into a narrow icon strip, and the decorative active-item indicator (sized for a wide menu) stuck out as a "drop" over the content. The menu was replaced with a standard off-canvas drawer.

Before → a narrow icon rail, a clipped circular indicator sticking out over the content, the menu ate into the width.

After → the menu is hidden behind a hamburger button (top left); a tap opens the full menu over the content with a dim overlay; the content uses the full width; no more artifact.

Repro steps

  1. Open the app on a phone (or narrow the window to ≈390px).
  2. You'll see: full-width content, a hamburger button top left, no side menu.
  3. Tap the hamburger → the full menu slides in from the left, the background behind it dims.
  4. Tap the dimmed overlay or a menu item → the menu closes.
  5. On desktop (>768px) the menu works as before — a side panel, the hamburger is hidden.

How it was built

  • A mobileOpen state in the sidebar; always off on desktop (hamburger hidden).
  • @media (max-width: 768px): the sidebar is taken out of flow (position: fixed, translateX(-110%)), sliding in when opened; a hamburger and a dim overlay were added; the content auto-expands to the full width.
  • The drawer shows the expanded panel with labels; the decorative rail indicator isn't rendered on mobile at all — the "drop" artifact is excluded in principle.
