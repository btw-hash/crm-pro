Saved Views — filter tabs above the pipeline

One click for the right slice of deals · the view is saved both in the URL and across sessions

What was built

  • A row of view tabs with counters now sits above the pipeline board:
    All5 My active4 No next step3 Critical1
  • Clicking a tab instantly filters the board; the active view is highlighted, with a counter shown.
  • The view is remembered: in the URL (?view=critical — can be shared) and in the browser across sessions.
  • "Critical" is tied to the Score Pill (#153), "My active" is my own open deals.

What each view shows

View What's in it
All all deals visible to you
My active assigned to you and not closed (not Won/Lost)
No next step deals with no planned next step — "easy to lose track of"
Critical red by Score Pill: stuck ≥14 days or long overdue on a next step

Why (vs a legacy CRM)

The head of sales can answer "where is the team stuck" in one click — "No next step" or "Critical". In a legacy CRM this has to be rebuilt with a filter every time. Comparable patterns: Pipedrive Filters, HubSpot Views, Linear views.

How to verify

  1. Open "Deals" — 4 tabs with counters are visible above the board.
  2. Click "Critical" → only red deals remain on the board; the URL becomes ?view=critical.
  3. Refresh the page / open in a new tab — the selected view persists.
  4. Views combine with the "Goal" filter — you can narrow it further.
