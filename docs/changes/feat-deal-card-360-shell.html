360 deal card — shell, stepper, bento (PR6, step 6a)

Before: the deal card /deals/[id] was a flat page: title, controls, a "Stage · Manager" line, and "Overview / Tasks" tabs. No stage stepper, no single visual focal point for "what to do now".

After: an XL bento layout matching the "CRM Pro UI" reference:

  • a back-link "← All deals";
  • a header band (glass): a "DEAL" chip + status chip ("In progress" / "No next step") + outcome badge + large title + manager avatar and name + current stage + "Open in ERP" button (for commercial roles);
  • a stage stepper following the real 6-stage pipeline (Qualification → Audit → Test request → Testing → Proposal → Completed): completed stages are dark with a checkmark, the current one is red, future ones are light with a number, connected by lines;
  • a row of stage actions: advance/revert stage, "More info", edit/delete;
  • a 2-column bento: on the left — a "What to do now" hero (next step / stalled-deal hint + AI advisor) and the stage sections; on the right — "About the deal" (fields, comment) and "Tasks and feed" (history + dictate/add task).

This is purely a visual shell: all forms, composers (audit/samples/report/proposal/order), stage controls, and RBAC are reused as-is. Rebuilding the workspaces (qualification, test→report→document, completion) is the next steps 6b–6e of epic #442.

The "5-step stepper vs 6-stage domain" fork (from #442) is resolved: the stepper is rendered from the domain's FUNNEL_TRACK / DEAL_STAGE_LABELS — a single source shared with the kanban, with no renaming of deal-stage.ts and no leakage into the board.
