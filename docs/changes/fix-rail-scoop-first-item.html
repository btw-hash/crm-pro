Fix for the "white tongue" around the icon on the collapsed rail

Before: on the collapsed sidebar, when the first rail item was active (Home / house icon), the active-section indicator drew a white "tongue" sticking up into the dark logo/hamburger zone. It looked like a broken artifact.

Cause: the "scoop" indicator is an SVG "hourglass" 140px tall: a waist cut-out around the icon in the middle, and wide cream-colored lobes above and below (the page background color #F8F3EF) that are meant to blend into the background along the rail's right edge. The scoop's offset = activeIdx*60 + 30 − 70. For the first item (activeIdx = 0) the offset = −40px → the upper lobe moves 40px above the rail rows, into the dark area — and the cream lobe reads as a white tongue against the dark background. It used to be clipped by the box edge; the animation fixes (#426/#429) left overflow:visible on the settled rail, and the lobe stopped being clipped.

After: the scoop is wrapped in a container with overflow:hidden sized to the rail rows' height — the upper lobe on the first item and the lower lobe on the last item are now clipped at the rail boundaries, leaving a clean cream crescent around the active icon. The floating disc and glyph are placed outside the wrapper — they still extend to the right past the rail edge. The fix is surgical, touching only app-sidebar.tsx.

Verification

  1. Open any screen as Head of Sales — the sidebar is collapsed to the rail by default.
  2. While on Home (house icon active, first rail item) — a clean crescent cut-out around the icon, no white tongue above the house icon (near the hamburger).
  3. Navigate to other sections (Clients / Catalog / Analytics) — the indicator moves to the item; at the edge positions the lobes also don't extend past the rail.
