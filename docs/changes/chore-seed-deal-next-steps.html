Demo data: "next step" on active deals

The /deals board looks like a working sales team, not an abandoned pipeline · #173

What was done

  • Seeded realistic planned next steps (open task-activities) so that most active deals already have a step set.
  • "Next step" = an open task (kind: "task", not completed) with copy tailored to the client and stage, and a due date (dueDate).
  • Due dates are intentionally varied: some are a few days out (healthy), a few are due today and overdue — the Score Pill and "Critical" view stay meaningful.
  • 7 active deals were deliberately left without a step — the "No next step" view and red pills keep showing up. Not everything is green.
  • Terminal deals (Won/Lost) were left untouched — they don't need a step.

How to reproduce

  1. A fresh dev start (not HMR — the seed loads into the in-memory container on startup): PORT=3018 npm run dev.
  2. Open /deals — most cards show a colored pill (green/yellow) and no "no next step" badge.
  3. Click the "No next step" view — a sensible remainder is left (qualifications, audits, a couple of tests), not the whole board.
  4. Click "Critical" — the red pill is still shown (a deal stuck at a stage for a long time).

Why

The demo board is the first thing a viewer sees. If almost every deal has "no next step", the CRM looks like nobody uses it, and the whole point of the "easy to lose track" signal is lost. A realistic seed shows a working team: most deals are being driven forward, and the ones slipping (red/yellow) stand out immediately — exactly the value the signal exists for. Only demo data (seed.ts) changed — logic and screens were left alone.
