Score Pill — deal health at a glance

A colored indicator on the pipeline card · the head of sales sees the whole team without a click

What changed

  • A colored "health" dot indicator now shows on the deal card in the pipeline: green · yellow · red. #153
  • Combines existing signals (days on stage + "no next step") into a single visual marker — no need to open a deal to see where it's stuck.
  • Terminal deals (Won/Lost) don't show the indicator.
  • The first step of the demo UX upgrade (see specs/demo-ux-upgrades.md).

Color rule

Color When Meaning
Green has a next step, on stage < 7 days on track, moving on time
Yellow no next step OR stalled ≥ 7 days needs attention
Red stuck ≥ 14 days OR no next step and already stalled critical — easy to lose

Why (vs legacy CRMs)

In a legacy CRM, you have to open a deal to see its state. Here the head of sales sees the "health" of the whole pipeline in 10 seconds right on the board — where it's green, where it's red. Comparable to Pipedrive Deal Rotting, HubSpot Deal Health.

How to verify

  1. Open the "Deals" section (the pipeline).
  2. To the left of each card's name is a colored dot. Hover over it → a tooltip explains the state.
  3. A deal with no next step / stuck on stage for a long time → a yellow or red dot.
  4. Drag a deal to "Won" → the dot disappears (the deal is closed).
