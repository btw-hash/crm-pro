End-to-end history, including deletions (#397 item 23)

Wraps up epic #397. Verified that key actions are recorded in history, and closed the one remaining gap — deletions.

What was already in history (verified)

  • Completed stages — a stage_changed / stage_reverted event on every transition.
  • Proposal — "Save to process" writes an entry to the "Task feed".
  • Audit, samples, test act, deal outcome, ERP order — each has its own event type (8 types).

What changed — deletions now have history

  • Soft delete: "Delete process" no longer erases the record — it sets a deletedAt marker. The process disappears from the board/lists/analytics, but the process itself and all of its history (events/activities) are preserved.
  • A new deal_deleted event ("Process deleted") — who deleted it and when.
  • A side-effect bug fix: previously a process with history couldn't be deleted at all (an FK error on child events). Now deletion always goes through.
  • An additive migration add_deal_deleted_at (nullable TEXT, 0_init untouched).

Repro steps

  1. Open a process (with activities/events) → "Edit" → "Delete process".
  2. The process disappears from the board and lists, and you're redirected to "Processes".
  3. The DB record is preserved with deletedAt; history shows a "Process deleted" event.
