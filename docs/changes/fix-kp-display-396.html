[21] Proposal prices are no longer "—"

Problem. At the "Proposal calculation" stage, in the "Proposal and letter (AI)" block, the Price, Monthly purchase, and Total columns showed "—" for every line item. Monthly volume and "Client savings" were computed correctly — only the prices were empty.

Cause. In build-proposal-draft.ts (line ~82) the price was taken only from the client's individual price (erp.getIndividualPrice). If the client doesn't have one — unitPrice = null → in the UI priceFmt ?? "—", lineTotalFmt ?? "—", totalFmt ?? "—" render dashes. There was no fallback to the catalog price.

Fix. Added a deterministic fallback to the product's RSP from the catalog (Sales Kit). The ERP's individual price still takes priority; RSP kicks in only when there's no individual price. The numbers aren't invented by the AI — RSP lives in the catalog data (seedProducts, present for all 9 demo products). 

// before
const unitPrice = individualPrice?.price ?? null;
// after
const unitPrice = individualPrice?.price ?? product.kit?.rsp ?? null;
Before After
Price / Purchase / Total "—" for every line item price from individual (if present) or catalog RSP; the total is computed
Caveat. RSP is the recommended retail price; for a B2B client the exact price comes from the ERP via getIndividualPrice. The ErpGateway port currently has no separate list/wholesale price, so RSP is the best available deterministic source for the demo (instead of "—"). With a real ERP the price will be correct automatically.

Repro steps

  1. Sign in as a manager, open a process (deal), get to the "Proposal calculation" stage.
  2. Select product(s), enter the quantity, click "Build proposal (AI)".
  3. Before: the Price / Monthly purchase / Total columns = "—". Now: a numeric price and total are shown (from the ERP's individual price or the catalog RSP).
