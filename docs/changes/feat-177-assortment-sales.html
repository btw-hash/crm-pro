Assortment analytics — sales analysis

A category manager's tool: what to clear out, what to hold, what to grow

What was done (closes #177, part of epic #176)

  • New "Assortment" section (category manager/head of sales role): for each item over the last 3 months — a "stop / hold / grow" signal based on the sales trend.
  • Dead stock (no sales for ≥ 3 months) → "✕ Stop — clear it out, don't reorder"; growth zone → "↑ Grow — order more"; stable items → "Hold".
  • Not naive ABC: ranked by gross margin (revenue × margin), not by units — cheap high-volume items don't sneak into the "top value" list.
  • The numbers are deterministic, from sales history, no AI call: prices and revenue never leave the trusted boundary (data guardrail #179).

Why

The category manager manually pulls together in the ERP what's selling, where stock is piling up, where they could buy more. The section produces that first cut automatically: it shows what's dead stock (clear it out, don't reorder) and what's growing (order more) — in real dollars and margin, not "by units".

How to verify

  1. Sign in as Alan Peterson (Head of Sales) or Buyer, open "Assortment".
  2. The table is sorted by value: Noirelle — rank #1 (≈ $16.4K gross margin), the most valuable items on top.
  3. Bakewell — "✕ Stop — clear it out, don't reorder · 6 months without a sale" (dead stock); Levara — "↑ Grow — order more" (growth zone).
  4. A counter-example to naive ABC: "Muffin cups" has the most units (15,750 units), but by value it's rank #8 — volume ≠ value.
