Home page: a livelier bento dashboard — tile animation + interactive charts

Issue #300 · branch feat/home-bento-interactive

What changed

  • Tiles appear with a gentle stagger on load (a smooth upward shift + fade-in, each with its own delay --i × 55ms) and lift on hover (hover-lift + a stronger shadow).
  • The "Tasks by stage" donut is interactive: hovering a segment (or its row in the legend) dims its neighbors, thickens the active segment, and shows that segment's count and label in the center instead of the grand total (drill).
  • Team pipeline: hovering a bar highlights it and shows the stage label next to the number.
  • Tooltips: on the conversion gauge (the exact "lead → task" value), on the team workload bars (load %), on the donut and pipeline segments.
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion: with the system's "reduce motion" setting on, the entrance animations and hover-lift are disabled.

Before → after

Before
  • Tiles all appeared at once (a single screen-wide .scr fade).
  • Charts were static: the donut/pipeline/gauge didn't react to the cursor.
  • Hovering a tile changed nothing.
After
  • Tiles cascade in, and lift on hover.
  • The donut drills down, the pipeline shows its stage, tooltips everywhere.
  • Motion turns off with the system's "reduce motion".

Repro steps

  1. Open / as the head of sales role (the default on localhost).
  2. On load, the tiles cascade in from bottom to top.
  3. Hover any tile → it lifts slightly with a shadow.
  4. Hover a segment of the "Tasks by stage" donut → its neighbors dim, the center shows the count and the stage's label.
  5. Hover a bar in "Team pipeline" → a highlight + the stage label next to the number.
  6. Hover the "Pipeline conversion" gauge arc and the team workload bars → tooltips.
  7. (Optional) Turn on "reduce motion" in the OS → reload: entrance and hover-lift animations are off, the data is unchanged.
