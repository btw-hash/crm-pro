New client onboarding

Document package collection → legal review → the client can be activated. A dedicated section /onboarding instead of "we agreed verbally."

What was built

  • A new "🤝 Onboarding" section (/onboarding): a new counterparty can't be activated until they've gone through document collection and legal review — a real operational step (contract work / data-privacy compliance) that used to live "in someone's head."
  • Package lifecycle with explicit statuses: "Collecting documents" → "Under legal review" → "Approved" / "Rejected (needs rework)". Transitions are only allowed legally (domain rules, not a free status change).
  • A standard package of 6 documents is created automatically (Company card, Certificate of good standing, Articles of incorporation, EIN certificate, Power of attorney for the signatory, Bank details). It can only be sent to legal once all of them are collected.
  • Legal review: a head of sales acting as the approver sees "Approve" / "Reject" buttons; on rejection they write a resolution (what needs to be fixed), the package goes back for rework and can be resubmitted.
  • Clean architecture: the onboarding domain (transition rules) + an OnboardingRepository port + a use case + an in-memory implementation behind the port (ready to be swapped for Postgres/ERP through the same interface). Transition logic is covered by domain tests.

Package lifecycle

Collecting documents → (all 6 collected) Under legal review → Approved
Under legal review → (legal rejects + a resolution) Rejected (needs rework) → resubmitted → Under legal review
The rules are strict: a package can be sent to legal only from "Collecting"/"Rework" and only once all documents are collected; it can be approved/rejected only from "Under review". Any other transition is rejected by the domain with an OnboardingError (a quiet "push through" of an incomplete package isn't possible).

Before → after

Before A new client was activated "based on a verbal agreement": legal review and the document set lived in conversations / email, with no trace in the system. It was unclear what stage a client was at or whether everything had been collected.
After An "Onboarding" section with a card for every new counterparty: you can see the status, which of the 6 documents are collected, a "Send to legal" button (once ready), and the legal resolution. A client's stage is a fact in the system, not something in a manager's head.

Access (RBAC)

  • The section is visible to head of sales / Manager / category manager roles (gated by canSeeMargin); a technologist doesn't need it.
  • The "Approve" / "Reject" buttons are only available to the approver (head of sales acting as the legal role, gated by canSeeTeamAnalytics) — a Manager submits the package but doesn't approve it themselves.

How to verify

  1. Open Onboarding (/onboarding) as head of sales.
  2. In the "New onboarding" form, enter the client's name (and optionally a tax ID), check off some of the documents → create it. The card appears with status "Collecting documents".
  3. While not all 6 documents are collected, there's no "Send to legal" button. Finish the package → "Send to legal" appears → the status becomes "Under legal review".
  4. As the approver — click "Approve" → status "Approved" (the client can now be activated). Or "Reject" with a resolution → status "Rejected (needs rework)", the resolution is shown on the card.
  5. Edge case: try creating an onboarding record with an empty name — the domain rejects it ("Enter the client's name").
